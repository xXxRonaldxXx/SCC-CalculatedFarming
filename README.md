# SCC: Calculated Farming

An add-on for **[SCC – Super Cool Calculator](https://github.com/xXxRonaldxXx/SuperCoolCalculator)**, for Minecraft **1.21.1 and 26.1.2 / NeoForge**. This repository is the **bug tracker** for it – the mod itself is published on CurseForge.

➡️ **[Report a bug or ask for a feature](../../issues/new/choose)**

> **Requires SCC 1.5.0 or newer.** The add-on does nothing on its own.

## What the add-on does

**Farms without fields – crops, trees and bees, calculated under a star-glass dome.**

A Growth Calculator keeps a little garden under a glowing dome and harvests it with the real drops of every plant in your pack: crops, trees, mushrooms, column plants and bees – with support for Mystical Agriculture, Productive Bees, Productive Trees and Productive Farming traits. Power and water come from the SCC network, the harvest goes straight into SCC storage. Six star tiers, no blocks placed in the world, no lag.

## Before you report

- The in-game manual explains every block and window.
- **`/scc_farming`** commands: `/scc_farming list`, `/scc_farming scan` and more.
- Problems that are about SCC itself (network, storage, autocrafting) belong in the **[SCC tracker](https://github.com/xXxRonaldxXx/SuperCoolCalculator/issues)**.

## What to put in a bug report

- Add-on version, SCC version, Minecraft/NeoForge version, and the modpack if you use one.
- **`logs/scc.log`** and `logs/latest.log` (or the crash report if the game crashed).
- What you did, what you expected, what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
